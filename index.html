Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email pluddy@outlook.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

My mug won't pair

Put the mug on its charging coaster, then press and hold the button on the bottom of the mug for about 5 seconds until the light flashes blue. Open Trivet, tap +, and wait for it to appear under "Nearby Mugs." Make sure Bluetooth is on and that Trivet has Bluetooth permission (Settings → Trivet).

It keeps disconnecting

Bluetooth range is short — keep the mug within a few metres of your iPhone. Trivet reconnects automatically when the mug is back in range.

I bought Pro but don't see it

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The short version: Trivet has no account and no analytics. It talks to your mug over Bluetooth on your device. The only time any data leaves your device is when you enable Live Activities, and then only to deliver those updates to your own device via Apple Push Notification service.

What Trivet stores on your device

  • Your paired mugs and their nicknames, temperature presets, and unit preference.
  • The most recent state of each mug (temperature, battery, fill), shared with Trivet's own widgets and Live Activities through a private app group.

This stays on your device (and in your iCloud device backup, if you use one). Trivet does not upload it. There is no server that stores your data.

Live Activities (push-to-start)

If you use Live Activities, iOS issues a push token for the activity. Trivet sends that token, together with the mug's current display state (name, temperature, battery, status), to a small stateless relay so Apple can start or update the Live Activity on your device. The relay validates and signs the request, then forwards it to Apple Push Notification service; it does not log or retain your tokens or mug data. This information is used only to render your own Live Activity and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or tracking.

What Trivet does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email, or contacts.
  • No advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, photos, or health data.

Purchases

Trivet Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. Trivet never sees your payment information.

Children

Trivet does not target children and collects no personal information.
